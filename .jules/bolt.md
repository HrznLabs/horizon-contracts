## 2024-07-17 - Storage caching in nested struct access
**Learning:** In Solidity, directly caching individual members of a storage struct (like `dispute.outcome`, `dispute.poster`) to local stack variables when they are accessed multiple times within a function (e.g., in complex conditional branches like `_distributeFunds`) measurably reduces gas consumption. This avoids the repeated calculation of the struct member's storage slot and the subsequent expensive `SLOAD` operations. Conversely, simply aliasing the struct to a `storage` pointer (e.g., `Dispute storage dispute = _disputes[disputeId];`) does not inherently avoid `SLOAD`s for the individual members.
**Action:** When a function makes multiple reads to specific fields within a storage struct, especially in loops or multiple `if`/`else` branches, explicitly cache those specific fields to memory/stack variables at the beginning of the function.
